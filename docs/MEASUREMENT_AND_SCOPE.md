# Measurement and scope qualifications

The frozen contract and report are historical execution records and are preserved exactly. The following clarifications were established during manuscript preparation and do not change any analysis.

1. The ±2.5-percentage-point margin was fixed before outcome calculation. Its recorded rationale was precision planning, not a validated minimum clinical or policy-important difference. Approximately 7% is the primary 95% CI upper limit divided by the prerule standardized rate; it is a descriptive scale comparison and not the basis used to choose the margin.
2. The annual core outcome HEARAID_A measures current hearing-aid use, without identifying FDA device class, acquisition date, initiation, or substitution. The 2023 sponsored item HRAIDAQR_A asks about acquisition route. It covers one year and is not an FDA regulatory-class flag; it was not analyzed under the completed frozen contract. Therefore a blanket statement that NHIS has no acquisition-related item would be too broad.
3. FDA authorized earbud-based hearing-aid software on September 12, 2024. The archived contract’s“pure OTC device period”label for 2023–2024 is too strong. Windows remain unchanged and 2025 stays separate.
4. Insurance changes are descriptive, with pointwise unadjusted CIs. Traditional Medicare classification does not exclude supplemental coverage. Changes in enrollment composition can contribute to contrasts; the results do not identify benefit coverage as the cause.
5. The industry panel’s approximately 4-point ownership contribution from OTC devices is a cross-sectional device-category contribution, not a before/after change in current use. It is not directly comparable to the NHIS period estimate.
6. The H definition includes hearing-aid users without residual difficulty. It is not a longitudinal cohort or a clinical definition of OTC eligibility. Independent person counts are distinct from imputation records.

The manuscript and author-declaration working files are maintained separately from this code/protocol release. Public repository availability does not establish a new institutional ethics approval or exemption. Original-survey oversight and secondary-analysis ethics are separate issues.
